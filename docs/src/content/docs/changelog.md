`igcp-aforro` ships under [CalVer](https://calver.org/) in the format `YYYY.MMDD.PATCH`. Monthly Euribor refreshes become `YYYY.MMDD.0` releases; same-day patch releases bump the trailing component (`.1`, `.2`, …).

The canonical changelog is the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/primor/igcp-aforro/releases), auto-generated by the release workflow on every `workflow_dispatch`. This page mirrors the high-level entries.

## Unreleased

- **Behavioural change — high-precision quarterly compounding**. `simulate()` no longer rounds to cents at each quarterly capitalization. The running balance is now kept in full decimal precision (`big.js`) through every capitalization, and cent quantization (banker's rounding) is applied only at serialization. This aligns the library's headline values (`currentValueNet`, `currentValueGross`, `totalInterest*`) with what **aforro.net** displays for booked certificates — for example, the Série E / 2023-03-29 / 5000-unit cohort now reports `5417.00` instead of `5417.02`. Existing consumers may see `simulate()` totals shift by up to a few cents on long-running cohorts. Per-row `schedule` cells remain independently-rounded display snapshots and may drift up to ±1 cent against the headline totals; the totals themselves stay the canonical reference. No public type shapes change.
- **Série E support**. The library now covers Certificados de Aforro Série E (Portaria n.º 329-A/2017, closed by Portaria n.º 149-A/2023) end-to-end, alongside Série F. Pass `series: 'E'` (or `Series.E`) to `simulate()`, `getCurrentRate()`, `getRateForCohort()`, and `getRateTable()`, or `--series E` to any `aforro` CLI command. Série E uses the `E3 + 1%` base-rate formula clamped to `[0%, 3.5%]`, a 10-year maturity, premium tiers of `+0.50%` (years 2–5) and `+1.00%` (years 6–10), and a `[100, 250000]` unit range. The `rates.json` artifact now ships both `series.E` and `series.F` blocks; `schemaVersion` stays at `1` (adding a new series is non-breaking).
